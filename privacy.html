Clipnote Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary

Clipnote does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything it does happens inside your browser.

What Clipnote reads

On youtube.com video pages, Clipnote reads the captions that YouTube already provides for the video you are watching, so it can build a summary and a searchable transcript. It also reads the current playback time so notes and key moments can jump to the right place.

What Clipnote stores

Your notes (text and timestamp, per video) and whether the panel is open are saved with Chrome’s local extension storage on your own computer. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling Clipnote deletes them.

AI summary

The optional AI summary uses Chrome’s built-in on-device AI. The transcript is processed on your device and is not sent to Clipnote or to any server run by Clipnote.

What Clipnote does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No servers: Clipnote has no backend and makes no network requests of its own beyond the caption data YouTube serves to the page.
  • No browsing history is read; Clipnote runs only on youtube.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/sintori2/clipnote/issues.
